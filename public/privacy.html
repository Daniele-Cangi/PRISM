Privacy Notice

Effective 19 August 2026.

What is processed

PRISM processes the public article URL you submit, extracted article text, and an IP-derived pseudonymous identifier used for abuse prevention. The guest interface does not create an account or use advertising cookies.

AI processing

Extracted article text is sent to the configured OpenAI API to provide the requested analysis. Do not submit private, confidential, personal, paywalled, or unlawfully obtained material.

Retention and logs

The application does not intentionally persist article text or generated analysis. Rate-limit identifiers expire after the configured window (24 hours by default). Submitted URLs and raw IP addresses are excluded from application logs. Infrastructure providers may maintain separate security records.

Contact

For the reference deployment, email info@unityloop.ai. Operators of other deployments are responsible for their own notice and data practices.
